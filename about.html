OUR STORY

The Story Behind Every Meaningful Gift

Founded in 2015 in the heart of Dubai's business district, GiftCraft was born from a simple belief: that a thoughtfully chosen gift has the power to transform a business relationship into a lasting partnership. What began as a boutique gifting consultancy for three anchor clients has grown into the UAE's most trusted corporate gifting powerhouse, serving Fortune 500 companies, government entities and fast-growing startups across the globe.

Our founders — a team of seasoned branding professionals and luxury goods specialists — noticed that most corporate gifting solutions prioritised volume over value, treating gifts as afterthoughts rather than strategic relationship investments. GiftCraft was created to change that narrative entirely.

Today, with a 180-person team, three production facilities and global logistics partnerships, we orchestrate over two million gifting moments annually. Each one is crafted with the same care and intentionality as the first gift we ever delivered.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

We Believe Gifting Is A Strategic Language

Three core principles guide every gift we design, source and deliver — from a single executive hamper to a 50,000-unit global programme.

Intentionality First

Every gift we create begins with understanding your relationship, your brand story and the emotional impact you want to create. We never recommend gifts by default — we craft with purpose.

Brand Precision

Your corporate identity is sacred. We obsess over colour matching, material quality and presentation so every gift feels like an extension of your brand — not a marketing add-on.

Relationship ROI

We measure success in retained clients, elevated employee engagement and strengthened partnerships — the tangible business outcomes that thoughtful gifting consistently drives.
