EXPERT TIPS

Professional Tips For Smart Corporate Gifting

Our senior gifting consultants share their most impactful advice for maximising the return on every corporate gift you send.

01

Plan your gifting calendar 3 months in advance for best pricing and product availability — especially for festive seasons.

02

Set a per-recipient budget and maintain consistency — it prevents awkward internal comparisons and ensures fairness across teams.

03

Personalise every gift with at minimum the recipient's name — data shows personalisation triples gift recall and strengthens the relationship signal.

04

Always consider cultural sensitivities, dietary requirements and religious observances — a misjudged gift can damage what a great one would strengthen.

05

Always include a handwritten or premium printed card — it's the gift within the gift, and the element recipients remember most.
